# COSCO Shipping — Carrier Profile and Documentation Requirements

## Carrier overview

**COSCO** (China Ocean Shipping Company) was founded in **1961 as a Chinese government agency** (first vessel Guanghua's maiden voyage to Jakarta, April 1961). It grew into one of the world's largest state-owned shipping and logistics suppliers, and in **January 2016 COSCO Group merged with China Shipping Group to form China COSCO Shipping Corporation** — a global container-shipping and terminals giant. COSCO's businesses span container shipping, terminals (including the COSCO Pacific/Hongkong International Terminals JV at Kwai Tsing since 1991), logistics (COSCO Shipping Logistics, from COSCO Logistics which handled agency, forwarding and 3PL since 2003), and bunkering (Chimbusco). In 1979, COSCO signed with Lykes Brothers Steamship Company the agreement that opened commercial sea routes between the United States and the People's Republic of China.

## Documentation requirements

| Document | COSCO practice |
| --- | --- |
| Booking confirmation | Issued with booking number, vessel/voyage, cut-offs |
| Shipping instructions | Portal/EDI submission before the SI cut-off; draft B/L confirmation |
| Bill of lading | Original negotiable, sea waybill, telex release per SI election |
| VGM | Per SOLAS VI/2 before the VGM cut-off |
| Dangerous goods | IMDG data at booking; DGD required (Chinese DG regulations additionally govern domestic legs) |
| China-specific | Export customs declaration through China Single Window; various certificates for China-origin goods (see the China customs guide in this corpus) |

## Notes for shippers

- Carrier for the world's largest export manufacturing base; intra-Asia and Asia–Europe/North America lane coverage is extensive.
- Documentation and SI processes mirror the international standard set (see the Shipping instructions and Bill of lading references).
- Terminal interests mean integrated port handling in many Chinese and overseas terminals.
- Verify current US/EU sanctions-related routing and booking rules where applicable to your cargo profile.
