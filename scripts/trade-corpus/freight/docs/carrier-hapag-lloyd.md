# Hapag-Lloyd — Carrier Profile and Documentation Requirements

## Carrier overview

**Hapag-Lloyd AG** is a German container shipping company based in Hamburg, formed from the **1970 merger of Hamburg America Line (HAPAG, founded 1847)** and **Norddeutscher Lloyd (NDL, founded 1857, Bremen)** — two of the oldest names in shipping. After the **2017 merger with United Arab Shipping Company (UASC)**, Hapag-Lloyd became the **world's fifth-largest container transporter** by vessel capacity. In **January 2024** it announced (with Maersk) the **Gemini Cooperation**, launching **February 2025** — after full implementation ~**340 ships / ~3.7 million TEU** (Maersk 60%, Hapag-Lloyd 40%) — while leaving THE Alliance in January 2025. In **February 2026**, ZIM Integrated Shipping Services was acquired for **US$4.2 billion** (per the ZIM reference).

## Documentation requirements

| Document | Hapag-Lloyd practice |
| --- | --- |
| Booking confirmation | Issued with booking number, vessel/voyage, cut-offs |
| Shipping instructions | Portal (Quick Quotes/SI workflows) or EDI before the SI cut-off; draft B/L verification in-portal |
| Bill of lading | Original negotiable, express (waybill-style), telex release per SI election |
| VGM | Per SOLAS VI/2 via portal/EDI before the VGM cut-off |
| Dangerous goods | IMDG data at booking, DGD required |
| Reefer data | Setpoints/instructions at booking for refrigerated cargo |

Hapag-Lloyd publishes country import/export guides and local charge tariffs on hapag-lloyd.com — pair with the customs country guides in this corpus.

## Notes for shippers

- Strong Atlantic, Latin America and Middle East coverage (UASC heritage).
- Under Gemini Cooperation, hub-and-spoke scheduling aims at high schedule reliability — verify transshipment implications for transit time and fees.
- Local demurrage/detention tariffs per location are published online; plan free time carefully (see the Freight charges reference).
