# CMA CGM — Carrier Profile and Documentation Requirements

## Carrier overview

**CMA CGM** (Compagnie Maritime d'Affrètement – Compagnie Générale Maritime) is a French container shipping and logistics group and the **third-largest container shipping company in the world**. The company has a presence in **160 countries with 400 offices and 750 warehouses, ~155,000 employees, and a fleet of 593 vessels**, serving **420 of the world's 521 commercial ports** on **257 shipping lines**. FY2024 revenue was **US$55.48 billion**. Group brands include **ANL** (Australia/NZ/Oceania–Asia trades) and **Comanav** (Morocco–Europe passenger/container), plus terminal interests through **CMA Terminals** and **Terminal Link** (ranked N°12 worldwide).

## Documentation requirements

| Document | CMA CGM practice |
| --- | --- |
| Booking confirmation | Issued per booking with booking number, vessel/voyage, cut-offs |
| Shipping instructions | Submitted through the carrier's eBusiness portal/EDI before the documentation cut-off; draft B/L confirmation in-portal |
| Bill of lading | Original negotiable, sea waybill, telex release, or electronic B/L per SI election |
| VGM | Per SOLAS VI/2 via portal/EDI before the VGM cut-off |
| Dangerous goods | IMDG data at booking; DGD required (see the Dangerous goods reference) |
| Reefer data | Setpoints/control instructions at booking for refrigerated cargo |

CMA CGM publishes import/export country guides (required documents, procedures, local charges) on its portal for major trades — pair them with the customs country guides in this corpus.

## Notes for shippers

- Strong Mediterranean, Africa, and French-speaking trades coverage (Comanav on Morocco routes).
- The group operates logistics brands (CEVA) alongside the liner, enabling door-to-door through one group.
- Local charges (THC, documentation, demurrage/detention) are port-specific and published by the local office — request them in writing with the quotation.
