# Yang Ming Marine Transport — Carrier Profile and Documentation

## Carrier overview

**Yang Ming Marine Transport Corporation** is a Taiwan-based container shipping company **founded on 28 December 1972**, tracing its heritage to the **China Merchants Steam Navigation Company established in Shanghai on 14 January 1873** (China's first self-operated shipping company, founded under Li Hongzhang's direction in the Tongzhi reign). The name "Yang Ming" commemorates the philosopher Wang Yangming. Yang Ming operates a fleet of container vessels and chartered ships on global East–West and intra-Asia trades, with bulk/PCC (pure car carrier) services in its history, and is a member of the major East–West alliance structures.

## Documentation requirements

| Document | Yang Ming practice |
| --- | --- |
| Booking confirmation | Issued with booking number, vessel/voyage, cut-offs |
| Shipping instructions | Portal/EDI submission before the SI cut-off; draft B/L confirmation in-portal |
| Bill of lading | Original negotiable, sea waybill, telex release per SI election |
| VGM | Per SOLAS VI/2 before the VGM cut-off |
| Dangerous goods | IMDG data at booking; DGD required |
| Reefer data | Setpoints at booking for refrigerated cargo |

Yang Ming publishes e-portal workflows (booking, SI, B/L, cargo tracking) and service schedules on yangming.com — pair with the customs country guides in this corpus.
