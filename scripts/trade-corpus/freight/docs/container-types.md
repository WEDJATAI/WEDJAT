# Container Types and Equipment — ISO Standard Units and Special Equipment

Intermodal containers are standardized by **ISO standard 668:2020** (dimensions/tolerances). About **90% of the global fleet are "dry freight"/general-purpose containers**: closed rectangular boxes of weathering steel, almost all **8 ft (2.44 m) wide**, of either **20 or 40 ft** standard length; standard heights are **8'6" (2.59 m)** and **9'6" (2.9 m) — the latter "High Cube" (HC/HQ)**. By end of 2013, high-cube 40-ft containers represented almost **50% of the world's maritime fleet** (Drewry Container Census). Capacity is counted in **TEU (twenty-foot equivalent units)**.

## Standard dry equipment

| Type | External | Typical internal | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 GP | 20' × 8' × 8'6" | ~2.34 m wide | Heavier cargo density |
| 40 GP | 40' × 8' × 8'6" | ~2.34 m wide | Volume workhorse |
| 40 HC | 40' × 8' × 9'6" | ~2.69 m high | Nearly half the world fleet |
| 45 HC | 45' × 8' × 9'6" | — | Pallet-max volume (regional acceptance) |

Regional variants: **pallet-wide (PW)** containers (~2.44 m internal width) load Euro-pallets (1.20 × 0.80 m) two-across-end-on or three side-by-side, vs ~2.00 m usable in standard ISO boxes; North American domestic **48-ft and 53-ft** boxes (APL introduced 48-ft in 1986; first ocean-capable 53-ft in 2007 — since discontinued on trans-Pacific by 2013, surviving in domestic/Caribbean trades).

## Special equipment

| Type | Use | Key checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Reefer (20RF/40RF/40RHC)** | Perishables — temperature-controlled | Setpoint, ventilation, PTI (pre-trip inspection) before gate-in; monitoring in transit |
| **Open top** | Oversized height cargo, crane loading from above | Tarpaulin condition, lashing points |
| **Flat rack** | OOG width/length machinery, project cargo | End-wall vs collapsible variants, lashing plan |
| **ISO tank** | Bulk liquids/gases (food, chemicals) | Tank operator acceptance, cleaning certificate |
| **Ventilated** | Cargo needing airflow (coffee, cocoa) | Vent calibration |
| **Garment on hanger (GOH)** | Hanging garments | Loading bars |
| **High-cube pallet-wide** | Max Euro-pallet density | Regional availability |

## FCL vs LCL

- **FCL (full container load)**: the merchant books a whole container; one packing list/B/L per box.
- **LCL (less than container load)**: cargo consolidated into a shared box by a consolidator/NVOCC; house bills per merchant over one master bill; CFS (container freight station) handling at both ends; charged per weight/volume (revenue ton).

## Practice notes

- Verify the container's **CSC plate** (safety approval) and condition at gate-in.
- For reefers: pre-cool cargo, book with setpoint + PTI, monitor via carrier reefer portals.
- For OOG: book flat rack/open top early (limited free supply), agree a lashing plan and obtain approvals.
- Payload: Max gross is typically 30,480 kg for 20'/32,500 kg for 40' — never exceed the VGM-based limits (see the VGM reference).
