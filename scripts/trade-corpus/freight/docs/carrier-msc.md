# MSC — Mediterranean Shipping Company Carrier Profile and Documentation

## Carrier overview

**Mediterranean Shipping Company S.A. (MSC)** was founded by Italian entrepreneur **Gianluigi Aponte and his wife Rafaela Aponte-Diamant in Italy in 1970**; it is owned by the Aponte-Diamant family and headquartered in **Champel, Geneva, Switzerland since 1978**. MSC is the **world's largest container shipping company by both fleet size and cargo capacity**, controlling **21.2% of global container capacity (November 2025)**. On 6 January 2022 MSC surpassed Maersk in TEU capacity. As of September 2025 it operates about **900 cargo vessels** with an intake capacity of **7,000,000 TEU**, sails over **215 trade routes** calling at **500+ ports**, runs **524 offices in 155 countries** with over **200,000 employees**, and operates vessels up to **24,346 TEU** (world's largest container ships, e.g. MSC Tükiye, MSC Michel Cappellini). The group also runs Medway rail freight (Iberia/Italy), MSC Cruises and cargo aircraft.

## Documentation requirements

| Document | MSC practice |
| --- | --- |
| Booking confirmation | Issued on booking acceptance with booking number and sailing/cut-off details |
| Shipping instructions | Submitted through MSC's e-portal/EDI before the SI cut-off; validate the draft B/L in the portal |
| Bill of lading | Original negotiable, sea waybill, or telex release per SI election |
| VGM | Per SOLAS VI/2 via portal/EDI before the VGM cut-off |
| Dangerous goods | DG data at booking; DGD per the Dangerous goods reference |
| Arrival notice / D/O | Destination agent issues arrival notice; release per document type |

MSC publishes country-specific import/export guides (documents, procedures, local charges) for its main trade lanes on msc.com — consult the guide for the destination country together with the customs country guide in this corpus.

## Notes for shippers

- As the largest line by capacity, MSC offers broad direct-call coverage; transshipment routings may add transit fees (see the Freight charges reference).
- Local origin/destination charges (THC, documentation fees, demurrage/detention tariffs) are published per port — request the full list with any quotation.
- For reefer cargo, setpoint and ventilation instructions travel with the SI/booking (see the Container types reference).
