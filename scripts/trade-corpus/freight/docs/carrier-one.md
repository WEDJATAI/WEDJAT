# ONE — Ocean Network Express Carrier Profile and Documentation

## Carrier overview

**Ocean Network Express Holdings, Ltd. (ONE)** is a Japanese container transportation and shipping company jointly owned by **Nippon Yusen Kaisha (NYK, 38%), Mitsui O.S.K. Lines (MOL, 31%) and K Line (31%)**. Launched in **2017** (operations 2018) as a joint venture, ONE inherited the container shipping operations of its parent companies — a combined fleet capacity of about **1.4 million TEU** — forming at that time the **sixth-largest container shipping company in the world**. The merger was part of the industry consolidation wave driven by poor profits and surplus capacity.

## Documentation requirements

| Document | ONE practice |
| --- | --- |
| Booking confirmation | Issued with booking number, vessel/voyage, cut-offs |
| Shipping instructions | Submitted through ONE's e-portal/EDI before the SI cut-off; draft B/L confirmation in-portal |
| Bill of lading | Original negotiable, sea waybill, telex release per SI election |
| VGM | Per SOLAS VI/2 via the portal/EDI before the VGM cut-off |
| Dangerous goods | IMDG data at booking; DGD required (see the Dangerous goods reference) |
| Reefer data | Setpoints at booking for refrigerated cargo (ONE's reefer fleet is significant on Asia–Oceania/Americas trades) |

ONE publishes country import/export guides, local charge schedules and free-time/demurrage-detention tariffs on one-line.com — pair with the customs country guides in this corpus.

## Notes for shippers

- Strong intra-Asia, Asia–Oceania and trans-Pacific coverage.
- Magenta-liveried fleet resulting from the three-parent merger; single-brand booking/SI/tracking portal consolidates the legacy lines' processes.
