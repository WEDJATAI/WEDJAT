# Forwarder Documents — FIATA FCR, FCT, FBL, FWR and FWB

FIATA (the International Federation of Freight Forwarders Associations) publishes a family of standardized forwarder documents used worldwide as marks of quality and harmonisation in freight-forwarder documents. A forwarder issuing them operates either as an **agent** (client's behalf, client's risk) or as a **carrier/principal** (own liability) — the document family covers both roles.

## The documents

| Document | Name | Function | Forwarder's role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **FCR** | FIATA Forwarders Certificate of Receipt | Non-negotiable certificate that goods have been received by the forwarder (with irrevocable handling instructions) — often used to trigger payment obligations when the buyer/seller arrangement substitutes it for the transport document | Agent (receipt only) |
| **FCT** | FIATA Forwarders Certificate of Transport | Certificate that goods have been dispatched/handed to a carrier, with the forwarder responsible for delivery to the named place under general stipulations | Agent with delivery undertaking |
| **FBL** | FIATA Multimodal Transport Bill of Lading | Negotiable multimodal transport document — the forwarder acts as **carrier (NVOCC/MTO)** with carrier liability under the FIATA rules; the house bill counterpart for door-to-door multimodal moves | Carrier/principal |
| **FWB** | FIATA Multimodal Transport Waybill | The non-negotiable counterpart of the FBL for multimodal transport | Carrier/principal |
| **FWR** | FIATA Warehouse Receipt | Receipt for goods taken into the forwarder's warehouse, with warehousing conditions (can be negotiable form) | Bailee |
| **SDT** | FIATA Shippers Declaration for the transport of dangerous goods | Shipper's DG declaration to the forwarder | — |
| (Related) | FIATA Forwarders Letter of Credit instructions etc. | Administrative aids | — |

The **General Stipulations** (FBL/FWB reverse terms) and the FCT conditions define the liability regimes; documents are obtainable through national forwarder associations (member federations) under FIATA's authorization system.

## FCR — the payment-trigger receipt

The FCR enables authorized freight forwarders to provide the consignor with an official acknowledgement that they have taken over the goods with irrevocable instructions — sellers commonly accept payment against FCR under sales terms that substitute FCR presentation for shipping documents (e.g. ex-works + FCR payment arrangements; commonly used in commodity/buying-office flows).

## House/master chains with FBL

In consolidation the forwarder issues the **FBL/HBL to the merchant** while the ocean carrier issues the **MBL to the forwarder** — the FBL is designed for exactly this NVOCC chain, giving the cargo interest a negotiable document against the forwarder's own carrier liability.

## When to use which

| Need | Document |
| --- | --- |
| Buyer pays on forwarder's receipt of goods | FCR |
| Proof of dispatch without negotiability | FCT (agent) or FWB (carrier) |
| Negotiable door-to-door document under forwarder liability | FBL |
| Warehouse custody documentation | FWR |
