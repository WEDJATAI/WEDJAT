# Evergreen Line — Carrier Profile and Documentation Requirements

## Carrier overview

**Evergreen Marine Corporation** was established on **1 September 1968** by Chang Yung-fa with one secondhand 15,000-dwt vessel, growing from the Evergreen Group's origins (the group designation covers Evergreen Marine, EVA Air and associated service companies, arising from 1975 diversification). Evergreen Marine operates **over 225 ships calling at 240 ports in about 80 countries** — ranked among the world's top container lines (the group reference lists it as the seventh-largest containerized shipping company, with earlier third-largest rankings in its history). Subsidiaries/divisions include **Uniglory Shipping Corporation, Hatsu Marine Ltd., and Italia Marittima S.p.A.**

## Documentation requirements

| Document | Evergreen practice |
| --- | --- |
| Booking confirmation | Issued with booking number, vessel/voyage, cut-offs |
| Shipping instructions | Portal/EDI submission before the SI cut-off; draft B/L confirmation in-portal |
| Bill of lading | Original negotiable, sea waybill, telex release per SI election |
| VGM | Per SOLAS VI/2 before the VGM cut-off |
| Dangerous goods | IMDG data at booking; DGD required |
| Reefer data | Setpoints at booking for refrigerated cargo |

Evergreen publishes shipment-document guidance and local tariffs on evergreen-marine.com — pair with the customs country guides in this corpus.

## Notes for shippers

- Strong Asia–North America and Asia–Europe lanes, with evergreen-liveried vessels and container fleets across the group brands.
- Documents follow the international standard set (see the Shipping instructions and Bill of lading references).
