# Container Terminals, THC, CY/CFS, Demurrage, Detention and Storage

## Terminal concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **CY (container yard)** | The terminal area where full/empty containers are held; CY-CY service = carrier's port-to-port responsibility |
| **CFS (container freight station)** | Where LCL cargo is consolidated/deconsolidated; CFS handling adds fees and time for LCL |
| **THC (terminal handling charge)** | The port-terminal fee for handling containers arriving/departing — charged at both ends; varies port-to-port with infrastructure, labour and equipment |
| **Gate-in / gate-out** | The container's entry/exit through the terminal gate; gate-in before the CY cut-off is mandatory for loading |

## Free time and the clock charges

- **Free time** — the number of days a container can be used free of charge; typical market ranges are around 7–10 days but vary by carrier, route and season. Expiry triggers:
- **Demurrage** — the fee for **using the container inside the port** beyond free time.
- **Detention** — the fee for **keeping the container outside the port** after pick-up (until returned empty). Unlike demurrage, detention starts after the container leaves the terminal; typical grace periods before it applies are short.
- **Port storage** — the terminal's own charge per additional day beyond grace, escalating with duration; distinct from (and additive to) demurrage in many ports.

Defense: schedule transport and customs clearance so the box moves before free time expires; every stage of shipment should be coordinated — carriers, customs and trucking.

## Chartering-side demurrage (bulk/tramp context)

In vessel chartering, demurrage is **liquidated damages owed by the charterer to the shipowner** when loading/unloading (laytime) exceeds the contract's allowance — the inverse being **despatch**. The master's **Notice of Readiness (NOR)** fixes when laytime starts (see the Port agents reference).

## Cost-planning checklist

1. Request the free-time terms and demurrage/detention tariffs per port with every quotation.
2. Check customs clearance lead time at destination against the free window (see the customs country guides in this corpus).
3. Budget THC at both ends (origin/destination) plus CFS fees for LCL.
4. Track empty-return deadlines after unpacking to stop the detention clock.
