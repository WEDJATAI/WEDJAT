# HMM — Carrier Profile and Documentation Requirements

## Carrier overview

**HMM Co., Ltd.** (formerly **Hyundai Merchant Marine**, rebranded **9 March 2020**) is a South Korean container transportation and shipping company. HMM moves the largest portion of **South Korea's exports** — the number-one Korean national container carrier, especially since **Hanjin Shipping's bankruptcy and liquidation**. Domestically HMM also transports strategic materials (crude oil, iron ore/coal, special products) alongside import/export goods; 2024 revenue was approximately **12 trillion Korean won**. As of 2020 HMM had built the world's two largest containerships by TEU capacity: **HMM Algeciras (23,964 TEU)** — the largest at her April 2020 launch — and **HMM Copenhagen (23,820 TEU)**.

## Documentation requirements

| Document | HMM practice |
| --- | --- |
| Booking confirmation | Issued with booking number, vessel/voyage, cut-offs |
| Shipping instructions | Portal/EDI submission before the SI cut-off; draft B/L confirmation in-portal |
| Bill of lading | Original negotiable, sea waybill, telex release per SI election |
| VGM | Per SOLAS VI/2 before the VGM cut-off |
| Dangerous goods | IMDG data at booking; DGD required |
| Reefer data | Setpoints at booking for refrigerated cargo |

HMM publishes e-portal workflows (booking, SI, B/L, VGM) and country guides on hmm21.com — pair with the customs country guides in this corpus.

## Notes for shippers

- The reference carrier for Korean-origin cargo (Korea–US/EU/SEA lanes), with ultra-large vessels on mainline Asia–Europe/North America services.
- Documentation practice follows the international standard set (see the Shipping instructions and Bill of lading references).
