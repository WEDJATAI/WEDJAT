# Jordan — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

Jordan is a civil-law jurisdiction. Companies are governed by the **Companies Law No. 22 of 1997 (as amended)**; the **Companies Control Department (CCD)** at the Ministry of Industry and Trade is the registrar. Foreign investment is generally open, with sector regulators (Central Bank, ASEZ authority) for specific activities.

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Limited Liability Company (LLC / ذ.م.م)** | Private limited | Limited | The most commonly established foreign form; single-shareholder LLCs permitted (with the Companies Registrar Commissioner's prior agreement per practice references) |
| **Private Shareholding Company (PSC)** | Private shareholding | Limited | The other most common Jordanian form (per Al Tamimi's practice overview) |
| **Public Shareholding Company (PSC/PLC)** | Public limited | Limited | Listed companies; minimum capital per regulation |
| **General Partnership** | Partnership | Unlimited | Natural persons |
| **Limited Partnership** | Partnership | Mixed | GP unlimited, LP limited |
| **Foreign company branch** | Branch | Parent liability | Registered foreign company operating for the parent |
| **Sole proprietorship** | Individual | Unlimited | Simple registration |

## Foreign company presence options

- **LLC or PSC subsidiary** — standard foreign-entry vehicles (100% foreign ownership permitted in most sectors).
- **Branch** — for companies with a parent contract/project in Jordan (registration with CCD).
- **Representative office** — promotional only.

## Registration process overview

1. Name reservation + approval at the **Companies Control Department**.
2. Memorandum & articles of association (notarized; Arabic) — founder signatures before the CCD.
3. Registration + **commercial registration**; tax number (Income & Sales Tax Department); chamber of commerce membership.
4. Sector licenses (ministries for regulated activities); municipality approvals.
5. Social Security Corporation (SSC) employer registration; work permits for foreign staff.

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- Importers register with the **Customs Department** (importer code) tied to the company's commercial registration; certain goods require prior import licensing through the relevant ministry.
- The **Aqaba Special Economic Zone (ASEZ)** offers its own licensing/customs regime for trading/distribution entities.
- Jordan's trade agreements (GAFTA, US-Jordan FTA, EU association) make certificate-of-origin structuring relevant (see the Certificate of origin reference).
