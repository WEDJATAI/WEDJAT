# Italy — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

Italy is a civil-law jurisdiction. The **Civil Code** (Codice Civile) governs companies and partnerships; registration runs through the **Registro delle Imprese** (companies register) at the local Chamber of Commerce (Camera di Commercio), with notarial deeds for capital companies. Startups/innovative SMEs have special registers.

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **S.r.l. (Società a responsabilità limitata)** | Private limited | Limited | The standard SME/trading form; ordinary or "semplificata" (single member, low capital) variants |
| **S.p.A. (Società per azioni)** | Company (≈ plc/corporation) | Limited | Larger companies; listed companies use the same form |
| **S.a.p.a. (Società in accomandita per azioni)** | Publicly traded partnership | Mixed | Limited partners + managing general partner |
| **S.n.c. (Società in nome collettivo)** | General partnership (commercial) | Unlimited | |
| **S.a.s. (Società in accomandita semplice)** | Limited partnership | Mixed | GP unlimited, LP limited |
| **S.s. (Società semplice)** | Simple partnership (non-commercial) | Unlimited | Agriculture/professional use |
| **Imprenditore individuale / lavoratore autonomo / libero professionista** | Individual | Unlimited | Sole trader, self-employed, professional |
| **Cooperative societies / social enterprises** | Cooperative | Members' | Special regulation and registries |

## Foreign company presence options

- **S.r.l. subsidiary** — the standard foreign-entry vehicle (100% ownership).
- **Branch (succursale)** of the foreign company — registered in the Registro delle Imprese; parent liability; suitable for smaller presences.
- **Representative office (ufficio di rappresentanza)** — promotional only.

## Registration process overview

1. Notarial **atto costitutivo + statuto** for S.r.l./S.p.A.; digital identity/spid formalities.
2. **Registro delle Imprese** filing via the notary (ComUnica single communication) — VAT + social security (INPS/INAIL) simultaneous registration.
3. Bank capital deposit before notary for capital companies.
4. **Agenzia delle Entrate** tax registration; sector licenses (municipality, SUAP one-stop for production activities).

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- Importers/exporters: **EORI** number via the Agenzia delle Entrate/customs; intra-EU trade under the VAT regime (reverse charge; INTRASTAT filings).
- S.r.l. is the default trading company; S.p.A. where capital-market/governance needs exist.
- The EU customs union means third-country imports arrive through Italian ports/airports with EU duty (see the Italy/EU customs guides in this corpus).
