# United Kingdom — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

The UK is a common-law jurisdiction (England & Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland systems). Companies are governed by the **Companies Act 2006** and registered at **Companies House**; tax (corporation tax, VAT) runs through HMRC. The UK left the EU single market/customs union (2021), making imports/exports to/from the EU customs-bounded.

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Limited company (Ltd)** — private company limited by shares | Private limited | Limited | The standard SME/trading form; one shareholder/director possible |
| **PLC (public limited company)** | Public limited | Limited | Minimum capital £50,000; may be listed |
| **Company limited by guarantee** | Not-for-profit | Limited | Members guarantee; charities/ associations |
| **Unlimited company** | Rare | Unlimited | |
| **LLP (limited liability partnership)** | Hybrid | Limited (members) | Professionals/ joint ventures |
| **Ordinary partnership** | Partnership | Unlimited joint | Partnership Act 1890 |
| **LP (limited partnership)** | Partnership | Mixed | Investment structures |
| **SLP (Scottish limited partnership)** | Partnership | Mixed | Scottish variant used in funds |
| **Sole trader** | Individual | Unlimited | Self-employed registration with HMRC |
| **CIC (community interest company)** | Social enterprise | Limited | Regulated asset-lock |

## Foreign company presence options

- **UK Ltd subsidiary** — standard foreign-entry vehicle (100% ownership; English incorporation; Companies House registration).
- **UK establishment (branch) of an overseas company** — registered at Companies House (OE form); trading possible; parent liability.
- **Representative office** — pre-trading liaison.

## Registration process overview

1. Choose name + **SIC code**; file **IN01** (online) with Companies House + memorandum/articles (model articles possible).
2. **Companies House number** issued; statutory registers (PSC — persons of significant control).
3. **Corporation tax registration** (HMRC, CT41G) + PAYE if employing; **VAT registration** over threshold/voluntarily.
4. Business bank account; sector licenses (FCA for financial services, etc.).

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- Importers/exporters: **EORI number** (GB-prefixed) required for customs declarations; imports from the EU need customs entries since Brexit; **CDS** replaced CHIEF for declarations (see the UK customs guide in this corpus).
- Ltd + VAT registration is the standard trader stack; duty deferment accounts and customs warehousing support trading flows.
- Documentary-credit business is well established in the London market (UCP practice).
