# United Arab Emirates — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

The UAE is a civil-law federation of seven emirates with **onshore (mainland)** companies governed by the federal **Commercial Companies Law — Federal Decree-Law No. 32 of 2021** (as amended), **free zone** companies governed by each free zone's own authority rules, and financial free zones (DIFC/ADGM) with common-law systems. Mainland licensing runs through each emirate's Department of Economic Development (e.g. Dubai DED), with federal registration (ministry of economy) and tax (FTA) interfaces.

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **LLC (Limited Liability Company)** | Private limited | Limited | The standard mainland form; since the 2021 reforms, onshore companies in many/most sectors may be **100% foreign-owned** (activities with strategic impact retain conditions) |
| **Private Joint Stock Company (PJSC)** | Private JSC | Limited | Less common |
| **Public Joint Stock Company (PJSC)** | Public company | Limited | Listed/larger companies |
| **FZE (Free Zone Establishment)** | Free-zone single-shareholder company | Limited | Governed by the rules of the free zone in which established |
| **FZC / FZ-LLC (Free Zone Company)** | Free-zone multi-shareholder company | Limited | Same free-zone governance |
| **Sole establishment / Civil company** | Individual / partnership | Unlimited (unless free-zone variant) | Simple mainland forms |
| **Branch of foreign company** | Branch | Parent liability | Licensed by the relevant authority (mainland via DED, or free zone) |
| **Representative/liaison office** | Office | Parent liability | Marketing/promotion only |

## Foreign company presence options

- **Mainland LLC** — 100% foreign ownership available across most activities post-2021; needed for onshore trading and government contracts.
- **Free zone company (FZE/FZ-LLC)** — fastest setup, customs-bonded advantages, but onshore trading requires a distributor or branch/dual licensing.
- **Branch / representative office** — as above; free zone companies are formally recognised as UAE companies under the Amendment Law (Article 13(3) context per Reed Smith).

## Registration process overview

1. Activity selection + **trade license** application (DED or free zone authority); initial approval + trade name reservation.
2. **Memorandum of association** (mainland) or establishment documents (free zone); notarization (mainland).
3. Tenancy (EJARI in Dubai) — physical office requirement.
4. License issuance; **commercial registration**; immigration establishment card; **visas**.
5. **Corporate tax (FTA) and VAT registration** where thresholds met; customs code registration for importers.

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- **Commercial licence** (Dubai DED) covers trading activities such as general trading, import/export, wholesale and retail — enabling mainland and free-zone operations (per Dubai's licensing guide).
- **Customs registration** (importer code) with the Federal/ emirate customs authority is required for import clearance.
- Free-zone entities importing into the mainland trigger customs duty (5% typical) unless via licensed dual-usage structures.
- EXPO/sector licenses and the UAE CEPA trade agreements affect duty planning (see the UAE customs guide in this corpus).
