# China — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

China is a civil-law jurisdiction. Mainland companies are governed by the **Company Law of the People's Republic of China** (revised **2024**, effective 1 July 2024); the **Foreign Investment Law (2020)** replaced the old three foreign-investment laws, putting foreign and domestic enterprises under one framework (with the FIE negative-list system). Registration runs through the **Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR)** with the Unified Social Credit Code; foreign projects file with MOFCOM/AMBER systems.

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **有限责任公司 (Limited Liability Company, LLC)** | Private limited | Limited | ≈ Ltd. (UK); the standard domestic and foreign-invested company form |
| **股份有限公司 (Joint-Stock Limited Company)** | Company ≈ plc/corporation | Limited | For larger/public companies |
| **个人独资企业 (Sole proprietorship)** | Individual enterprise | Unlimited (single natural-person owner) | |
| **合伙制企业 (Partnership)** | Partnership | Per GP/LP type | General and limited partnership forms |
| **个体工商户 (Individual industrial/commercial household)** | Micro-registration | Personal | Small-scale local business |
| **WFOE (Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise)** — now a standard LLC | LLC with 100% foreign capital | Limited | The classic foreign-invested vehicle |
| **FICE (Foreign-Invested Commercial Enterprise) — trading WFOE** | WFOE licensed for trading | Limited | May trade, wholesale, retail or franchise in China |
| **Joint Venture (EJV/CJV)** | LLC/cooperative with foreign+Chinese capital | Limited | Historically required in restricted sectors |

## Foreign company presence options

- **WFOE (LLC)** — the default foreign vehicle. The three common WFOE types:
  - **Consulting/Service WFOE** — services;
  - **Trading WFOE (FICE)** — import/export, wholesale, retail, franchising (requires physical office address as registered address);
  - **Manufacturing WFOE** — production (factory premises).
  Under the 2024 Company Law all registered companies face strengthened capital-contribution and governance rules.
- **JV (equity/cooperative)** — where the negative list requires a Chinese partner.
- **Representative office (RO)** — liaison only; cannot invoice/trade.
- **Free-trade-zone variants** — FTZ registrations streamline sectors like trading and finance.

## Registration process overview

1. Name pre-approval + SAMR registration (articles of association; Unified Social Credit Code).
2. **Company chops/seals** (official seal, finance, legal representative).
3. Bank account (RMB + foreign currency, capital account for WFOE); capital contribution schedule per the 2024 Company Law.
4. **Tax registration** (State Taxation Administration); customs registration for traders (see below); social insurance accounts.
5. Sector licenses (e.g., food/business licenses for trading activities; import/export rights registration).

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- To import/export directly, a company registers **customs declaration rights** (import-export operators file with customs/ MOFCOM historic registration, now integrated); production-type foreign-invested enterprises acquire import-export and declaration rights with their approval certificate (per Chinese tax/advisory references).
- **FICE/trading WFOE** is the required structure for foreign companies that want to buy/sell domestically (wholesale/retail), vs. service WFOEs which cannot trade.
- Chinese subsidiaries of trading groups typically maintain separate customs-registration, export-VAT (rebate) and foreign-exchange (SAFE) compliance tracks (see the China customs guide in this corpus).
