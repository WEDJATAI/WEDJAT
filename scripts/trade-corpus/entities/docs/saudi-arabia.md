# Saudi Arabia — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

Saudi Arabia is a civil-law jurisdiction with a modernized **Companies Law** (the new Companies Law, in force 2023, restructured the company forms and introduced new vehicles). Foreign investment is licensed through the **Ministry of Investment (MISA, formerly SAGIA)**, with registration at the Ministry of Commerce and the ZATCA tax authority.

## Main entity forms

| Form (Arabic) | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **LLC — شركة ذات مسئولية محدودة (Limited Liability Company)** | Private limited | Limited | Most common SME/investment vehicle |
| **Simplified Joint Stock Company (SJSC)** | New 2022/2023 form | Limited | Flexible startup/VC vehicle introduced by the new Companies Law; supports founder agreements, vesting-style arrangements |
| **JSC — شركة مساهمة (Joint-Stock Company)** | Public-style company | Limited | Larger companies; listed companies |
| **شركة تضامن (General Partnership)** | Partnership | Unlimited joint | Natural persons |
| **شركة التوصية البسيطة (Limited Partnership)** | Partnership | Mixed | GP unlimited, LP limited |
| **مؤسسة فردية (Individual Establishment)** | Sole proprietorship | Unlimited (single owner) | Simple registration |
| **شركة أجنبية (Foreign Company / branch)** | Branch of foreign entity | Parent liability | MISA-licensed |

## Foreign company presence options

- **LLC with 100% foreign ownership** — allowed in most activities under the MISA licensing regime; trading (wholesale/retail) activities historically required higher capital commitments (references cite up to SAR 30 million for 100%-foreign trading activities — confirm current MISA rules per activity).
- **Branch office** of the foreign parent — executes the parent's contracts; MISA license required.
- **Representative office** — promotion/liaison only.
- **Regional HQ (RHQ) program** — licensed regional headquarters incentives.

## Registration process overview

1. **MISA foreign-investment license** for the chosen activity.
2. **Ministry of Commerce** name reservation and articles via the unified portal.
3. Notarization, commercial registration (CR), chamber of commerce membership.
4. **ZATCA** tax registration (VAT where applicable), GOSI social insurance for staff, municipality licenses.
5. Sector regulators (SFDA for food/drug, CITC/ CST for telecom, etc.).

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- A foreign-owned **trading company** must be appropriately licensed for import/distribution activities via MISA + Ministry of Commerce; customs registration follows with the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA).
- **SABER/SASO conformity** requirements apply to regulated goods regardless of entity form (see the Inspection certificates reference).
- SJSC suits tech/startup structures; LLC remains the default for trading houses.
