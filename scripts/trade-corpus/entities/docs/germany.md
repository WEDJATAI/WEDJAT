# Germany — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

Germany is a civil-law jurisdiction. Corporate law is anchored in the **Handelsgesetzbuch (HGB, Commercial Code)**, the **GmbH-Gesetz** and the **Aktiengesetz**; companies register with the **local commercial register (Handelsregister)** at the competent court (Amtsgericht), with mandatory notarization and strict disclosure/accounting standards. The EU company forms (SE) are also available.

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **GmbH (Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung)** | Private limited | Limited | The SME standard; minimum share capital **€25,000** (half payable on formation) |
| **UG (haftungsbeschränkt)** — Unternehmergesellschaft | "Mini-GmbH" | Limited | Low-capital GmbH variant (often €1) with capital-reserve duties |
| **AG (Aktiengesellschaft)** | Public limited | Limited | Minimum capital €50,000; the listed-company form |
| **KGaA** | Partnership limited by shares | Mixed | Hybrid listed form |
| **GbR (Gesellschaft bürgerlichen Rechts)** | Simple partnership | Unlimited | No minimum capital; non-commercial/small |
| **OHG (Offene Handelsgesellschaft)** | General (commercial) partnership | Unlimited | GbR carrying a Handelsgewerbe |
| **KG (Kommanditgesellschaft)** | Limited partnership | Mixed (GP unlimited, LP limited) | With combined forms: **GmbH & Co. KG** (GP is a GmbH) — the classic German Mittelstand structure |
| **Einzelunternehmen / e.K. (e.Kfm./e.Kfr.)** | Sole trader / registered merchant | Unlimited | Individual businesses |
| **e.V. (eingetragener Verein)** | Registered association | Limited | Non-commercial purposes |
| **SE (Societas Europaea)** | EU company | Limited | Supranational form |
| **Freiberufler (professionals)** | Individual/partnership | Varies | Professional-services regulation |

## Foreign company presence options

- **GmbH subsidiary** — the standard foreign-entry vehicle (100% foreign ownership permitted).
- **Zweigniederlassung (branch)** — registered in the Handelsregister; parent remains liable; full trading possible.
- **unselbständige Zweigniederlassung (dependent office)** — coordination only.

## Registration process overview

1. Notarized **Gesellschaftsvertrag** (articles) + managing-director (Geschäftsführer) appointments.
2. **Handelsregister** filing (elektronischer Handelregister) — HRB number for the GmbH.
3. Bank capital account; **Steuernummer** + **USt-IdNr** (VAT ID) at the Finanzamt; **Wirtschafts-Identifikationsnummer**.
4. Trade office (**Gewerbeanmeldung**) for commercial activity; chamber (IHK) membership is automatic.
5. Sector licenses (e.g. **Bewachungsgewerbe**, financial services under BaFin).

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- Import/export within the EU single market: intra-EU acquisitions/reverse-charge VAT; outside the EU: customs import via the **ATLAS** system, EORI number requirement (see the Germany/EU customs guides in this corpus).
- GmbH & Co. KG is widely used for family/founder-controlled trading firms; GmbH for subsidiaries.
- Export-control (dual-use) compliance sits with the exporter entity (BAFA licensing — see the Import and export licenses reference).
