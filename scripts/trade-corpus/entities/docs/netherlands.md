# Netherlands — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

The Netherlands is a civil-law jurisdiction (Dutch Civil Code Book 2). All non-governmental legal entities register with the **Kamer van Koophandel (KVK, chamber of commerce)** business register; company tax and VAT registration run through the Belastingdienst. The NV/BV reforms (flex-BV since 2012) removed minimum-capital barriers for the BV.

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **BV (besloten vennootschap)** | Private limited | Limited | ≈ Ltd/LLC; flexible capital since the flex-BV reform; **the standard trading/company form** — single shareholder allowed; shares not publicly tradable |
| **NV (naamloze vennootschap)** | Public limited | Limited | ≈ plc/corporation; minimum issued capital **€45,000**; "nameless" shares; the listed form |
| **Coöperatie (cooperative)** | Cooperative | Member liability variant (WA/BA/UA) | May distribute to members; common for international holding/group structures |
| **Onderlinge waarborgmaatschappij** | Mutual insurance society | Variant | |
| **Vereniging (association)** | Association | Variant | May trade; no profit distribution |
| **Stichting (foundation)** | Foundation | Limited | May run a business; **no profit distributions** to founders/board |
| **VOF (vennootschap onder firma)** | General partnership | Unlimited joint | Multi-owner trading firm |
| **CV (commanditaire vennootschap)** | Limited partnership | Mixed | GP unlimited, silent partners limited; open/closed tax variants |
| **Maatschap (Mts)** | Professional group practice | Partners | Professionals sharing facilities |
| **Eenmanszaak** | Sole trader | Unlimited | Individual business |
| **vve** | Homeowner association | Statutory | Apartment owners' association |

## Foreign company presence options

- **BV subsidiary** — the standard foreign vehicle (100% ownership; English-language incorporation supported).
- **Branch (filiaal)** — registered at the KVK under the parent's name; no separate legal personality but full trading capacity; parent liable.
- **Representative office** — non-trading liaison.
- Dutch **Coöperatie** holding structures are common for international groups (participation exemption).

## Registration process overview

1. Statutory deed with a **civil-law notary** (BV/NV) — notarial formation.
2. **KVK registration** (inschrijving) — extracts (uittreksel) issued; no legal personality without notarial deed where required.
3. **BSN/rsin** + VAT (btw) identification via the Belastingdienst.
4. Sector permits (environment, food — NVWA — etc.).

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- Importers/exporters: **EORI number** (customs) + VAT registration; the Netherlands' port infrastructure (Rotterdam) and bonded logistics (customs warehousing) make the BV a common European distribution/trading entity.
- Intra-EU trade: no customs borders, VAT reverse-charge / ICV filings; imports from outside the EU enter via the Dutch customs systems (see the Netherlands customs guide in this corpus).
