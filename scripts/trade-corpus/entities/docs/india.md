# India — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

India is a common-law jurisdiction. Companies are governed by the **Companies Act 2013** (administration by the **Ministry of Corporate Affairs, MCA**, through the Registrar of Companies); LLPs by the LLP Act 2008; partnerships by the Partnership Act 1932. Foreign investment follows the FDI policy (automatic/approval routes with sectoral caps).

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Private Limited Company** | Private company | Limited | **2–200 shareholders**; shares held privately, cannot be offered to the public; the standard SME/FDI vehicle |
| **Public Limited Company** | Public company | Limited | More than 200 shareholders; can be listed or unlisted |
| **One Person Company (OPC)** | Single-member private company | Limited | Introduced by the Companies Act 2013 |
| **Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)** | Hybrid | Limited | Registration mandatory; ≥2 designated partners (director-equivalents) |
| **Partnership** | Partnership | Joint and unlimited | Registration not compulsory (Registrar of Firms); active vs sleeping partners |
| **Sole Proprietorship** | Individual | Unlimited | Simplest form; PAN/Aadhaar/bank/office proof |
| **Hindu Undivided Family (HUF)** | Family entity | Karta-managed | Joint family property/business with the Karta at its head |
| **Branch / Liaison / Project Office** | Foreign presence | Parent-linked | RBI/FEMA approval routes |

## Foreign company presence options

- **Private Limited subsidiary** — the standard FDI vehicle (100% foreign shareholding in most sectors under the automatic route).
- **LLP** — permitted in limited sectors.
- **Branch office / liaison office / project office** — RBI routes for specific purposes (liaison cannot trade).

## Registration process overview (company route)

1. **Digital signatures (DSC)** and **DIN** (director identification) via MCA.
2. **Name reservation (SPICe+ / RUN)** through the MCA portal.
3. Incorporation filing (SPICe+): memorandum & articles, declarations — certificate of incorporation with CIN.
4. **PAN/TAN** allotment (integrated), bank account, registered office.
5. **GST registration** where turnover/activity requires; shops & establishments; sector licenses.

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- **IEC (Importer-Exporter Code)** — a key business identification number, **mandatory for export from India or import to India**, issued by **DGFT** (Directorate General of Foreign Trade) against the company's PAN.
- Trade structuring: private limited company + IEC + GST registration is the standard importer/exporter stack; AD (Authorized Dealer) bank code registration links the company's bank account to customs filings.
- Duty assessment: Indian customs duty is computed against the **CIF value** of goods (see the Incoterms reference) — contracts commonly specify CIF/CFR accordingly.
