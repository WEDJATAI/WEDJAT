# Spain — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

Spain is a civil-law jurisdiction. Companies are governed by the **Ley de Sociedades de Capital** (Capital Companies Law); registration runs through the **Registro Mercantil** with notarial deeds, coordinated with the **CIRCE/PAE one-stop digital incorporation**. The EU company form (SE) and regional variants (e.g. Catalan cooperative forms) also exist.

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **S.L. (Sociedad Limitada)** — SL | Private limited | Limited | ≈ Ltd; minimum capital **€3,012**; the SME standard; single-member (SLU) variant |
| **S.L.N.E. (Sociedad Limitada Nueva Empresa)** | New-Enterprise SL | Limited | One-day fast-track incorporation (introduced 2003); minimum capital €3,012 |
| **S.A. (Sociedad Anónima)** | Public limited | Limited | ≈ plc; minimum capital **€60,101.21**; the listed form |
| **S.Com. p.A. (Sociedad Comanditaria por Acciones)** | Partnership limited by shares | Mixed | |
| **S.C. (Sociedad Colectiva)** | General partnership | Unlimited | |
| **S.Cra. (Sociedad Comanditaria)** | Limited partnership | Mixed | |
| **C.B. (Comunidad de Bienes)** | Co-ownership partnership | Members | Simple joint-ownership vehicle |
| **S.C.P. (Sociedad Civil Privada)** | Private civil partnership | Members | |
| **S.L.L. (Sociedad Limitada Laboral)** | Labour limited company | Limited | Employee-owned majority |

## Foreign company presence options

- **S.L. subsidiary** — the standard foreign-entry vehicle (100% ownership).
- **Branch (sucursal)** — notarial deed + Registro Mercantil registration; parent liability.
- **Representative office** — promotional only.

## Registration process overview

1. **NIE/NIF** for foreign shareholders/directors; name clearance (certificación negativa).
2. Bank capital deposit; notarial **escritura** (deed of incorporation) with statutes.
3. **Registro Mercantil** filing; **NIF** (tax ID) from the Agencia Tributaria.
4. Census declaration (modelo 036/037); Social Security (TGSS) employer code; municipal licenses.

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- Importers/exporters: **EORI** + VAT; intra-EU reverse charge and INTRASTAT; third-country imports through Spanish customs (see the Spain/EU customs guides in this corpus).
- S.L. is the default trading company; fast SLNE registration suits small startups.
- S.L.L./cooperative forms support employee-owned structures.
