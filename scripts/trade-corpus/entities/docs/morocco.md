# Morocco — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

Morocco is a civil-law jurisdiction (French legal heritage). Companies are governed by **Law 17-95 (sociétés anonymes)** and **Law 5-96 (SARL and other forms)**, operating under the Commercial Code umbrella. Registration runs through the **OMPIC** (central commercial register / directeurs des registres) and the **CRI (Centre Régional d'Investissement)** one-stop regional centers; tax through the DGI.

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SARL (Société à responsabilité limitée)** | Private limited | Limited | **Morocco's most widely used structure** (Law 5-96); single-shareholder formation permitted; capital freely set by the associates (statutory minimums low/historically symbolic) |
| **SA (Société anonyme)** | Joint-stock company | Limited | Governed by **Law 17-95**; board or directoire governance; larger companies |
| **SAS (Société par actions simplifiée)** | Simplified joint-stock | Limited | Modern flexible form available in Morocco |
| **SCA (Société en commandite par actions)** | Partnership by shares | Mixed | GP unlimited, LP limited |
| **SNC (Société en nom collectif)** | General partnership | Unlimited | |
| **SCS (Société en commandite simple)** | Limited partnership | Mixed | |
| **Succursale (branch)** | Branch | Parent liability | Foreign company registered locally |
| **Auto-entrepreneur** | Sole-trader micro regime | Unlimited | Statutory micro-entrepreneur status |
| **SARL AU / personne physique** | Individual with limited option | Varies | |
| **GIE / EIG (groupement d'intérêt économique)** | Economic interest grouping | Members | Consortium vehicle |

## Foreign company presence options

- **SARL subsidiary** — the standard foreign-entry vehicle (100% ownership permitted in most sectors).
- **Branch (succursale)** — registered with the CRI; suitable where the parent contracts locally.
- **Representative/bureau** — promotional only.
- **Casablanca Finance City (CFC)** status for regional HQ/finance structures; **Tanger Med / free zones** for export processing (customs advantages).

## Registration process overview

1. **CRI one-stop**: negative certificate (name) from OMPIC; statutes drafted (notary for some forms).
2. Bank capital deposit (SARL/SA); **tax ID (identifiant fiscal)**; commercial register (OMPIC) filing.
3. **CNSS** (social security) employer registration; professional tax; municipality license.
4. Sector licenses; import/export registrations for traders (customs code — see the Morocco customs guide in this corpus).

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- Importers/exporters register with customs (via the BADR/PORTNET ecosystem) against the company's tax ID; an **importer's license/attestation** applies to certain products.
- Morocco's FTAs (EU association, US FTA, African agreements, Tanger Med's export platform) reward origin-planning (see the Certificate of origin reference).
- SARL remains the default for SME trading; SA/SAS for larger or investor-facing ventures.
