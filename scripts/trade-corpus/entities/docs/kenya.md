# Kenya — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

Kenya is a common-law jurisdiction. Companies are governed by the **Companies Act No. 17 of 2015** (a modern consolidation), administered by the **Business Registration Service (BRS)** under the Attorney General's office (eCitizen/ Business Registration Service portal); tax through the KRA (Kenya Revenue Authority) with the **KRA PIN** as the universal tax identity.

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Private company limited by shares (Ltd)** | Private limited | Limited | The standard SME/trading form; single-member companies permitted |
| **Public limited company (PLC)** | Public limited | Limited | May be listed on the NSE |
| **Company limited by guarantee** | Not-for-profit | Limited | Membership guarantee |
| **LLP (limited liability partnership)** | Hybrid | Limited | Under the LLP framework |
| **Partnership (registered)** | Partnership | Unlimited | Registration of firms route |
| **Sole proprietorship (business name)** | Individual | Unlimited | Business-name registration at BRS |
| **Branch of a foreign company** | Branch | Parent liability | Registered as a foreign company (Companies Act part on external companies) |
| **Cooperative society** | Cooperative | Members | Under the Cooperatives framework |

## Foreign company presence options

- **Private limited subsidiary** — the standard foreign-entry vehicle (100% ownership generally permitted; sectoral limits in some services).
- **Branch of a foreign company** — registered with the BRS as an external company; parent guarantees/ liability.
- **Representative office** — promotional.

## Registration process overview (company route)

1. **Name search & reservation** at BRS (eCitizen).
2. Registration forms: **CR1** (application), **CR2** (model memorandum for a private company), **CR8** (directors' residential address notice), plus statement of nominal capital and particulars of directors/shareholders/secretary (with national ID/postal address/email/phone details for shareholders, directors and company secretary).
3. Certificate of incorporation; **KRA PIN** (obligatory for the company and directors); VAT registration where applicable.
4. Sector licenses (county single business permit; sector regulators — KEBS standards, pharmacy board, etc.).

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- Importers/exporters: KRA PIN + customs registration on the **Simba system / new customs platform**; **Kenya TradeNet** single-window handles import permits and declarations for regulated agencies (see the Kenya customs guide in this corpus).
- The private limited company is the default trading structure; branches trade under the parent's name with local registration.
- EAC (East African Community) customs union context affects tariff structuring.
