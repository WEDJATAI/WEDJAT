# Turkey — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

Turkey is a civil-law jurisdiction. Companies are governed by the **Turkish Commercial Code No. 6102 (TCC, in force 1 July 2012)**; simple partnerships by the Code of Obligations (Act No. 6098). Registration runs through the **Trade Registry (Ticaret Sicil)** attached to chambers of commerce (MERSIS central system); foreign investment is freely permitted with notification-based procedures.

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ltd. Şti. — Limited şirket (Limited Company)** | Private limited | Limited | Max **50 shareholders** (TCC art. 574/1); minimum capital **TL 50,000**; registered shares only (min par TL 25) |
| **A.Ş. — Anonim şirket (Joint-stock company)** | Company (≈ plc/corporation) | Limited | Minimum capital **TL 250,000**; bearer or registered shares (min par TL 0.01); **the only form that can be publicly traded** |
| **Koll. Şti. — Kollektif şirket (Collective/General partnership)** | Partnership (with legal personality) | Unlimited | Natural-person partners |
| **Kom. Şti. — Komandit şirket (Commandite partnership)** | Partnership | Mixed (GP unlimited, LP limited) | Simple or share-divided (sermayesi paylara bölünmüş) variants |
| **Adi ortaklık** | Simple partnership | No legal personality; contractual | Code of Obligations vehicle |
| **Koop. — Kooperatif (Cooperative)** | Cooperative | Members' liability per statute | Cooperatives Act No. 1163 |
| **Sole proprietorship (Şahıs şirketi)** | Individual | Unlimited | Simple registration |
| **HAAO (Halka Açık Anonim Ortaklık)** | Publicly held corporation | Limited | Special A.Ş. under the Capital Markets Act No. 6362 |

## Foreign company presence options

- **Ltd. Şti. or A.Ş. subsidiary** — 100% foreign ownership permitted; incorporation via MERSIS + Trade Registry; minimum capital rules above.
- **Branch (şube)** of the foreign company — registered with the Trade Registry; same-liability extension of parent; requires appointed representative.
- **Liaison office** — non-commercial (market research/coordination); Ministry approval.

## Registration process overview

1. **MERSIS** online name reservation and article drafting.
2. Notarized signature declarations; Trade Registry petition, chamber registration.
3. Capital blocked account deposit (bank) and certification; **tax number / potential VAT registration** at the local tax office.
4. Workplace opening approval (municipality) + social security (SGK) employer registration.
5. Sector licenses (e.g., import/export registrations where required; see the Turkey customs guide in this corpus).

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- Importers/exporters: companies register with customs (via the UBM system integration) as part of tax registration; e-export/e-import structures run through the same entity.
- The Ltd. Şti. is the standard SME trading form; A.Ş. for larger/capital-markets-facing ventures.
- Turkish Commercial Code brought additional obligations for capital companies (board composition, independent audit thresholds).
