# France — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

France is a civil-law jurisdiction (Code de commerce). Companies register through the **Guichet Unique (INPI one-stop shop**, formerly CFE/Greffe)**; the **RNE (Répertoire National des Entreprises)** replaced the RCS registers stream. Government guidance sets out the main forms' features; the **SAS is the most common commercial form** today.

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SAS (Société par actions simplifiée)** | Simplified joint-stock | Limited | **Most common commercial form**; flexible statutes; single-member (SASU) variant |
| **SARL (Société à responsabilité limitée)** | Private limited | Limited | Classic SME form; EURL single-member variant |
| **SA (Société anonyme)** | Public limited company | Limited | Larger/listed companies |
| **SNC (Société en nom collectif)** | General partnership | Unlimited | |
| **SCS (Société en commandite simple)** | Limited partnership | Mixed | GP unlimited, LP limited |
| **SCA (Société en commandite par actions)** | Partnership limited by shares | Mixed | |
| **EI (Entreprise individuelle)** | Sole proprietorship | Unlimited | With option for limited-liability patrimony (EIRL historically) |
| **Micro-entreprise / Auto-entrepreneur** | Sole-trader micro regime | Unlimited | Revenue-capped simplified framework |
| **Profession libérale / SEL** | Professional practice | Varies | Sociétés d'exercice libéral = incorporated practices |
| **GIE (Groupement d'intérêt économique)** | Economic interest grouping | Members | Consortium vehicle |
| **Association (loi 1901)** | Non-profit | Members | May trade under conditions |

## Foreign company presence options

- **SAS or SARL subsidiary** — standard foreign-entry vehicles (100% ownership; SAS preferred for flexibility).
- **Succursale (branch)** — registered like a local company; parent liability.
- **Bureau de liaison** — non-trading liaison.

## Registration process overview

1. Statutes drafted; **capital deposit** (bank/ notary) for capital companies; single-member variants possible.
2. **Guichet unique INPI** filing — company creation (immatriculation), SIREN/SIRET issuance; former RCS for commercial companies.
3. Tax (impôt sur les sociétés or personal) + VAT registration integrated.
4. Sector licenses; social-security (URSSAF) registrations for employers.

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- Importers/exporters: **EORI** + SIRET/VAT; intra-EU acquisitions/reverse charge; third-country imports via French customs (see the France/EU customs guides in this corpus).
- SAS suits trading subsidiaries with founder/ investor flexibility; SARL remains familiar for traditional SMEs.
