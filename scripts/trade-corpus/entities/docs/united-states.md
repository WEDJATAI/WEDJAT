# United States — Company Types and Legal Entity Structuring Guide

## Entity landscape overview

The US is a common-law **federal system**: business entities are created under **state law** (each state's corporation/LLC statutes — Delaware's are the most used for larger companies), with federal overlays (federal tax classification via the IRS "check-the-box" rules, securities law, and federal licensing for specific activities). There is no single national companies registrar; formation happens at the state level (Secretary of State offices).

## Main entity forms

| Form | Legal form | Liability | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **LLC (Limited Liability Company)** | Hybrid (members) | Limited | The dominant small/medium business form; flexible taxation (disregarded/partnership/corporate election); state-series LLC variants exist |
| **Corporation (C-Corp)** | Company (Inc.) | Limited | Shareholder ownership; federal corporate tax; standard for venture/ publicly traded companies |
| **S-Corporation** | Tax election of a corporation | Limited | Pass-through federal taxation with eligibility limits (shareholders/citizenship/classes) |
| **General Partnership (GP)** | Partnership | Unlimited joint | Agreement-based |
| **Limited Partnership (LP)** | Partnership | Mixed (GP unlimited, LP limited) | State filing; used in investment/real-estate structures |
| **Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)** | Partnership | Limited (partner-shield variants by state) | Professional firms mainly |
| **Sole Proprietorship / d/b/a** | Individual | Unlimited | Assumed-name registration at county/state level |
| **Nonprofit corporation; Cooperative** | Special forms | Varies | 501(c)(3) etc. |

## Foreign company presence options

- **US LLC or corporation subsidiary** — any foreign person/entity may own one; EIN from the IRS; state registration.
- **Branch (foreign corporation qualification)** — registering the foreign parent to do business in a state ("foreign qualification").
- **Representative office** — informal; no separate US status.

## Registration process overview (company/LLC)

1. Choose the state; **articles of incorporation / certificate of formation** filed with the Secretary of State; registered agent requirement.
2. **Employer Identification Number (EIN)** from the IRS (needed for bank, tax, hiring).
3. Operating agreement / bylaws (internal governance, not always filed).
4. Business licenses (state/county/city; sector licenses), sales-tax registration where the state imposes it.
5. Federal sector registrations (FDA facilities, ATF, DOT, etc. where applicable).

## Trade-specific structuring notes

- **Importer of Record (IOR)**: the US importer registers with CBP via a **customs bond** and an **EIN/ customs assigned number**; the IOR is liable for duties and declarations (entry filings by customs brokers under the broker's power of attorney).
- **EEI/AES filing** (Census) is required for most exports above value thresholds — filed against the USPPI's EIN (see the US customs guide in this corpus).
- Delaware LLCs/corporations are common holding/trading vehicles; nexus/sales-tax and state registration follow actual business footprint.
