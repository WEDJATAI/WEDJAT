# Germany — Import and Export Customs Documentation Guide (EU)

## Customs administration

Germany is in the **EU Customs Union**: the **Union Customs Code (UCC)** — substantive provisions effective **1 May 2016** — is the legal framework, applied by the **Generalzolldirektion/ customs (Zoll)** through the **ATLAS** electronic system. The EU is a single customs territory: one import clearance covers movement throughout the Union; intra-EU trade needs no customs declarations (VAT-only regime).

## Import procedure overview

1. **ENS pre-arrival filing** by the carrier.
2. Import declaration in ATLAS (broker/ importer with EORI); tariff classification in the EU TARIC/ German 8-digit CN-based codes (see the HS reference).
3. Valuation (transaction value; Value Declaration where > €20,000), preference/ origin evidence.
4. **Customs duty (third-country rate) + import VAT (19% standard German rate)**; excises where applicable.
5. Release; goods move freely within the EU.

## Export procedure overview

1. Export declaration (ATLAS/AES); risk exit clearance.
2. BAFA license for controlled items before shipment.
3. Preference certificates for destinations with EU FTAs.
4. Exit confirmation; export-VAT zero-rating documentation.

## Duty and tax framework

- EU **common external tariff** (TARIC) on CIF; German **import VAT 19%** (reduced rates on some goods) with input-VAT deduction; excise (energy, alcohol, tobacco) at clearance.
- Special procedures: customs warehousing, inward processing, temporary admission, **AEO** trusted-trader benefits.

## Special requirements and trade programs

- **EORI** registration (BZSt) mandatory for all customs operations.
- Intra-EU acquisitions/reverse-charge VAT replace customs duties inside the Union.
- German export-control (AWV/ dual-use) reporting duties on many transactions.
