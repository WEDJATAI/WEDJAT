# United Arab Emirates — Import and Export Customs Documentation Guide

## Customs administration

The **Federal Customs Authority (FCA)** sets policy, while each emirate's customs department executes (Dubai Customs, Abu Dhabi Customs, Sharjah etc.) on the **GCC Common Customs Law** framework. Declarations run through emirate portals (e.g. Dubai's **Mirsal 2**); the UAE uses harmonized HS-based tariffs with GCC unified rates, and the **UAE Trade Net/ single-window** ecosystem routes permits. Free zones have their own customs procedures (bonded).

## Import documentation requirements

| Document | Required for | Issuing/checking authority | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial invoice | All imports | Exporter | Detailed; trade.gov lists invoice + packing list among standard set |
| Certificate of origin | Imports (origin-sensitive goods) | Chamber of commerce in the country of origin, approved | Required per trade.gov; since the 2019/2020 reform **importers may declare origin on the invoice** in qualifying cases instead of a separate COO (EY tax news) — but keep COO available (Dubai Customs may require proof of origin to verify) |
| Detailed packing list | All | Exporter | |
| Bill of lading / air waybill | All | Carrier | |
| Import permit/ license | Restricted goods (food/health/telecom) | Sector regulators (MoIAT, municipalities) | Product-specific |
| Conformity certificates (ECAS/ESMA) | Regulated products | MoIAT/ EQM scheme | Electrical, cosmetics, etc. |
| Delivery order + customs declaration | Clearance | Customs portal | Registered importer code required |

Importer must hold a **trade license** with import activity and a **customs code** (see the UAE entities guide).

## Export documentation requirements

| Document | Required for | Authority | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Export declaration | All exports | Customs portal | |
| Commercial invoice + packing list | All | Exporter | |
| Certificate of origin | Per destination | Chamber of commerce | GCC/ GaFTA forms; **origin attestation** for UAE-exports under several FTAs |
| Export permits | Controlled goods | Regulators | |
| Free-zone movement documents (D/O/ transfer) | Free-zone exports | Free zone authority + customs | Free-zone declarations + duty on entry to mainland (5% unless exempt) |

## Import procedure overview

1. Shipment arrives; manifest filed by carrier; declaration submitted by the broker/ importer on the portal.
2. Classification (HS), valuation (CIF), permit validation (single-window routing to regulators).
3. **Duty 5% of CIF for most goods** (Dubai Customs: alcohol and cigarettes excepted at higher rates) + **VAT 5%** on CIF + duty.
4. Payment; release; delivery order; gate-out (documents retained **5 years** from clearance per Dubai Customs).

## Export procedure overview

1. Export declaration; commercial docs issued.
2. Certificate of origin (chamber) for preference claims; permits for controlled goods.
3. Loading; export statistics; FTA origin documentation where the destination has an agreement (CEPA network, GaFTA etc.).

## Duty and tax framework

- **Customs duty 5% CIF** standard (GCC common tariff); specific goods higher (tobacco, alcohol) or exempt.
- **VAT 5%** (since 2018) on imports (CIF + duty); exports zero-rated.
- **Free zones**: imports into free zones are generally duty-suspended; **entry into mainland** triggers the 5% duty unless the goods qualify under exemptions; documents and duty-payment records (or proof of free-zone storage) retained 5 years.
- Excise tax on carbonated/energy drinks, tobacco, e-liquids.

## Special requirements and trade programs

- **Free zones vs mainland** structuring drives the customs flow (see the UAE entities guide) — free-zone-to-mainland sales are imports.
- **CEPA agreements** (India, Türkiye, Indonesia, Israel, and others) offer preferential duty on qualifying origin — pair with certificates of origin.
- Dubai specifics: **Mirsal 2** declarations; **AED** valuation; banned/restricted lists enforced via the single window.
