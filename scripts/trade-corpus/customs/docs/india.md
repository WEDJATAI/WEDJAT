# India — Import and Export Customs Documentation Guide

## Customs administration

Indian customs sits under the **Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC)**; electronic filing runs through **ICEGATE** (Indian Customs Electronic Gateway). The **Importer-Exporter Code (IEC)** issued by **DGFT** is a key business identification number **mandatory for export from or import to India**; GST registration integrates duty/tax assessment.

## Import procedure overview

1. Importer (IEC + GST + ICEGATE registration with DSC) files the **Bill of Entry** before/ on arrival (electronic, via ICEGATE).
2. Risk assessment (RMS: facilitation vs. examination).
3. Classification (Indian Customs Tariff — 8-digit HS extension; see the HS reference) and valuation (**CIF-based assessment** — see the Incoterms note).
4. **BCD (basic customs duty) + IGST (standard 18%/ others) + social welfare surcharge** computed via ICEGATE.
5. Duty payment (GST credit offsets IGST), release, out-of-charge.

## Export procedure overview

1. Shipping Bill filed on ICEGATE (let export order after checks).
2. LEO (let export order); export proceeds realized through the AD bank (export-declaration linkage).
3. GST zero-rating: LUT/ bond route or refund against the shipping bill; duty-drawback/ RoDTEP claims.

## Duty and tax framework

- **BCD** per tariff (HS-based), **10% social welfare surcharge** on BCD, **IGST** at the goods' rate (5–28% slabs) on CIF + duty; GST credit usable.
- India computes duty against the **CIF value** (Incoterms-sensitive contracts).
- Schemes: RoDTEP, duty drawback, SEZ/ EOU regimes, Advance Authorisation.

## Special requirements and trade programs

- **ICEGATE/ ICEGRAVITY** self-registration; DSC mandatory.
- FTAs (ASEAN, UAE CEPA, Australia etc.) with CO evidence.
- **SCOMET** dual-use export controls (DGFT licensing).
- **AEO** trusted-trader certification speeds clearance.
