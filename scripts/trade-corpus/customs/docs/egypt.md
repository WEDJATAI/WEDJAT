# Egypt — Import and Export Customs Documentation Guide

## Customs administration

The **Egyptian Customs Authority (ECA)** under the Ministry of Finance administers clearance; the **General Organization for Export and Import Control (GOEIC)** controls importer/exporter registration and conformity. Egypt operates the **NAFEZA** national single window with the **ACID (Advance Cargo Information Declaration)** system — electronic ACI filing has been **mandatory for sea freight since 1 October 2021**, and from **1 January 2026 it is also required for air shipments**. The ACID number is generated by NAFEZA once the Egyptian importer files the pre-arrival data; exporters upload shipping documents to the platform through **CargoX** (blockchain document-transfer provider) so goods can be released on arrival without delay.

## Import documentation requirements

| Document | Required for | Issuing/checking authority | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial invoice | All imports | Exporter; legalized | **Two copies plus the original** required; **legalization of origin is required in most cases**; chamber certification + Egyptian Embassy legalization for documents |
| Certificate of origin | Most imports | Chamber of commerce at origin | Two copies plus original; preferential forms (GaFTA/EU association etc.) for duty claims |
| Bill of lading / air waybill | All shipments | Carrier | Match ACID data |
| Packing list | All shipments | Exporter | Per the standard set |
| Form 4 import declaration | Foreign-exchange-linked imports | Central Bank of Egypt framework | Historical FX allocation mechanism |
| ACID registration | Sea (and, from 2026, air) | NAFEZA + CargoX upload | Pre-clearance documents: commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin — uploaded via CargoX |
| Import permits/licenses | Controlled goods (food, pharma, telecom…) | Sector regulators (NFSA, EDA, NTRA…) | Product-specific |
| GOEIC conformity/registration | Regulated consumer goods | GOEIC | Factory registration + product registration/testing/COC (per published GOEIC requirements) |
| Inspection certificates | Designated goods | GOEIC/ inspection agencies | Pre-shipment or destination inspection |

Importer eligibility: registration in the commercial register + **GOEIC importer registration**; natural-person importers must be Egyptian nationals with tax cards (per GOEIC's published rules).

| Document | Required for | Authority | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Export declaration | All exports | Customs (single window) | Via NAFEZA |
| Commercial invoice + packing list | All | Exporter | |
| Certificate of origin | Per destination/contract | Chambers / GOEIC-linked | GaFTA, EU, COMESA forms as applicable |

| Export permits | Controlled goods (antiquities, strategic goods) | Sector ministries | |

## Import procedure overview

1. **Before shipping**: importer registers (GOEIC), files ACID data on NAFEZA, receives the ACID number, shares it with the exporter.
2. Exporter uploads invoice/packing list/B/L/CO to **CargoX** against the ACID before arrival.
3. Vessel departs/arrives; customs risk-assesses via the ACID dossier.
4. Declaration lodged; classification/valuation checks; **duties + VAT paid** (see below).
5. Release after GOEIC/sector checks; delivery order issued (see the Arrival notice reference).

## Export procedure overview

1. Exporter card + commercial registration; export declaration through NAFEZA.
2. Goods presented; customs examines if flagged.
3. Certificate of origin issued (chamber + GOEIC practices) for the destination's preference claims.
4. Loading; B/L issued; documents couriered/credited per the sales contract.

## Duty and tax framework

- **VAT: standard rate 14%** — applies to all goods and services except machinery/equipment for production lines (which enjoy relief tracks), with reduced and 0% categories (PwC/ trade.gov summaries).
- **Customs duties**: Presidential Decree No. 419/2018 raised tariffs on 300+ goods, modifying duties across **5,700+ HS tariff lines** (trade.gov) — the Egyptian tariff follows the HS structure (see the HS nomenclature reference); rates vary by HS chapter.
- **Free zones** (Investment Law framework): project imports/exports to/from abroad are **not subject to normal customs procedures or import rules**; customs exemptions apply to imports/exports and goods manufactured within the zone (UNCTAD/ GAFI/ law-firm summaries). Raw materials and equipment enter duty-free under investment incentives.

## Special requirements and trade programs

- **ACID/NAFEZA/CargoX** — the defining Egyptian requirement (dates above); non-registered cargo risks refusal/ delays.
- **GOEIC factory + product registration** for exporting manufacturers to Egypt (registration application, commercial-registry certificate, product testing, certificate of conformity — per GOEIC-published requirements).
- Legalization chain: chamber of commerce at issuance → **Egyptian Embassy legalization** for most origin documents.
- Preferential agreements: **GaFTA** (Arab states), EU–Egypt Association, **COMESA**, QIZ ( Qualified Industrial Zones with US duty-free access for Israeli-content goods), Mercosur and EFTA agreements.
