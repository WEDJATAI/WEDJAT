# Turkey — Import and Export Customs Documentation Guide

## Customs administration

The **Ministry of Trade (Ticaret Bakanlığı)** administers customs through the **Turkish Customs Enforcement (Gümrük Muhafaza)/ customs administrations**; declarations run through the **e-Gümrük/ BİLGE** electronic system. Turkey is a **EU Customs Union** member (for industrial goods) and operates EFTA/FTA networks; the **TAREKS** single-window risk system screens imports with product-safety modules.

## Import procedure overview

1. **Summary declaration before arrival** — submitted before goods arrive at Turkish customs territory (Ministry of Trade guidance).
2. Goods presented; **customs declaration (gümrük beyannamesi)** submitted electronically (istanbullawyerfirm: imports run through the electronic declaration).
3. TAREKS risk screening; document checks (CO/A.TR, conformity, control certificates).
4. Assessment: customs duty (EU industrial goods: none per Customs Union; others per tariff), VAT (standard 20%), other levies (SMP mass-housing fund on some goods).
5. Payment and release; post-clearance audit rights.

## Export procedure overview

1. Export declaration via e-Gümrük (the exporter or broker).
2. Commercial docs + preference certificates (A.TR for EU-bound industrial goods; EUR.1/ FTA COs).
3. Loading/ exit confirmation; export proceeds tracking via banks (foreign-exchange declaration context).

## Duty and tax framework

- **EU Customs Union**: industrial goods from the EU enter duty-free (A.TR); third-country goods pay the common external-tariff-based rates on the Turkish schedule (HS-based — see the HS reference).
- **VAT**: standard rate 20% on imports (with reduced/exempt categories).
- Additional funds/levies apply to specific goods (e.g., mass-housing fund on certain electronics) — verify per HS line.

## Special requirements and trade programs

- **TAREKS** product-safety single window for consumer/industrial imports.
- **Control certificates** for agricultural/animal products (see table).
- Preferential networks: EU Customs Union, EFTA, and bilateral FTAs (check each destination's form).
- Inward/temporary processing regimes and free zones support trading structures (see the Turkey entities guide).
