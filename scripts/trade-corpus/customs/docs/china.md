# China — Import and Export Customs Documentation Guide

## Customs administration

The **General Administration of Customs of China (GACC)** administers customs; declarations run through the **Single Window (单一窗口)** with the Import/Export Declaration Forms (each numbered per customs district per GACC rules). Specialized regimes: **GACC Decrees 248/249** govern imported-food registration; product regulators (SAMR, CCC certification) interface with customs clearance.

## Import documentation requirements

| Document | Required for | Issuing/checking authority | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Import declaration form | All imports | GACC Single Window | Numbered per customs district (GACC) |
| Commercial invoice + packing list | All | Exporter | Customs verification includes **documents proving the actual situation of the goods — name, specifications, model** (FAS/GACC text) |
| Bill of lading / air waybill | All | Carrier | |
| Certificate of origin | Preferential/ trade-remedy cases | Issuing country's authority | GACC/ customs issue China's own COs; inbound COs support preference claims |
| **GACC registration (Decree 248/249)** | Imported food | GACC | Imported food must meet the registration conditions to legally clear — **without a valid GACC registration number shipments cannot clear customs** regardless of quality/agreements |
| CCC certificate | CCC-catalog products | CCC bodies | Product-safety market entry |
| Inspection/ quarantine certificates | Animals/plants/food | GACC (CIQ function) + origin authority | Health/phyto/veterinary certificates |
| Import licenses | Restricted goods | MOFCOM | |

## Export documentation requirements

| Document | Required for | Authority | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Import procedure overview

1. Importer (registered with customs — see the China entities guide) or broker files the declaration on the Single Window.
2. GACC risk-inspects: documents, inspection, quarantine as applicable (food: Decree 248/249 registration is a precondition).
3. Classification (Chinese 8–10-digit HS extension of the universal 6 digits — see the HS reference), valuation.
4. Duty + import VAT (13%/9% typical standard rates) + consumption tax where applicable.
5. Release; post-clearance audit under the verification framework.

## Export procedure overview

1. Export declaration via Single Window (export rights required).
2. CO issuance (CCPIT or customs) for preference/ contractual needs.
3. Loading; export statistics; VAT-rebate filing against the declaration.

## Duty and tax framework

- MFN/ interim rates per the Chinese tariff schedule (HS-based; China applies 8–10 digit national codes).
- **Import VAT 13%** (9% for certain goods), collected at clearance with duty; consumption tax on luxury/ specific goods.
- FTA networks (ASEAN-China RCEP, etc.) grant preferential rates with CO evidence.

## Special requirements and trade programs

- **GACC Decree 248/249** food-registration regime — the decisive food-import gate.
- **CCC** product certification for catalog goods.
- **Single Window** consolidation; e-port card (USB key) for the trader's declarations.
- Free-trade zones (pilot FTZs) with bonded logistics (see the China entities guide).
