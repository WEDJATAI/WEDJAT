# Saudi Arabia — Import and Export Customs Documentation Guide

## Customs administration

The **Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA)** administers customs; the **FASAH** single-window platform carries declarations. **SABER** (run under the Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization — SASO) manages product conformity certification for regulated goods. A Saudi **commercial registration is required to open a customs file with ZATCA** and submit import declarations (the importer of record must be a registered Saudi entity — see the Saudi entities guide in this corpus).

## Import documentation requirements

| Document | Required for | Issuing/checking authority | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial invoice | All imports | Exporter | Presented to ZATCA with the entry |
| Bill of lading | Sea imports | Carrier | |
| Certificate of origin | Imports (trade-agreement claims) | Chamber of commerce | Trade.gov: ZATCA requires importers to present invoice, B/L and certificate of origin |
| SABER certificate (Product CoC + Shipment CoC) | Regulated products | SABER/SASO-conformity bodies | Demonstrates compliance with Saudi technical regulations; **without valid Saber certificates** regulated goods are blocked |
| SASO/inspection certificates | Designated goods | SASO-appointed agencies | Product-specific |
| Import licenses/permits | Controlled goods (food/SFDA, telecom/CST…) | Sector regulators | SFDA food/drug; CST telecom approvals |
| Customs declaration | All | Broker on FASAH | Registered importer file with ZATCA |
| Insurance certificate | Where applicable | Insurer | Duty/VAT computation on CIF |

| Document | Required for | Authority | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Export declaration | All exports | FASAH/ZATCA | Export customs service fees waived (0%) per current practice |

## Import procedure overview

1. Importer (Saudi-registered entity) opens the customs file with ZATCA.
2. Before shipment: SABER product/shipment certification for regulated HS codes; sector permits.
3. On arrival: declaration on FASAH; ZATCA risk-assesses; classification/valuation.
4. **Duty + VAT**: standard customs duty **5% of CIF for most goods** (excise/ higher rates for specific lines); **VAT 15% calculated on CIF plus duty**; shipments below **SAR 1,000** duty-free per express-practice references (VAT may still apply — verify with ZATCA).
5. Payment and release; delivery order (see the Arrival notice reference).

## Export procedure overview

1. Export declaration through FASAH (fees waived per current practice).
2. Commercial docs issued; certificate of origin from the chamber (GaFTA/ GCC preference forms).
3. Loading; carrier documents; export statistics captured by ZATCA.

## Duty and tax framework

- Customs duty: generally **5% on CIF**; higher protective rates for specific goods; **Arab/GCC trade agreements** may reduce (e.g. GaFTA preferences).
- **VAT 15%** (import VAT on CIF + duty; exports are zero-rated in principle — confirm treatment with ZATCA).
- Excise tax on soft drinks/energy drinks/tobacco (50–100%).

## Special requirements and trade programs

- **SABER/SASO conformity** is the defining Saudi import requirement — register products, obtain PCoC (product) + SCoC (shipment) certificates (see the Inspection certificates reference).
- **FASAH** single window for declarations; **ZATCA e-invoicing (FATOORA)** integration for the import entity's tax side.
- GCC unified customs union: single-entry duties across GCC states; Saudi standards (SASO/SFDA) as gatekeepers.
- Preferential regimes: **GaFTA**, GCC agreements; the KSA–US trade relationship has no FTA (standard MFN).
