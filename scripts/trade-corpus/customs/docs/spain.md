# Spain — Import and Export Customs Documentation Guide (EU)

## Customs administration

Spain is in the **EU Customs Union**: the **Union Customs Code** framework is applied by the **Department of Customs and Special Taxes (Departamento de Aduanas e Impuestos Especiales)** within the **Agencia Tributaria** — declarations are submitted at the **Agencia Tributaria Electronic Office** (SIA/ import-export declaration systems). **EORI** identifies traders; the SAD is the standard EU declaration model.

## Import procedure overview

1. ENS pre-arrival; arrival at Spanish port/ airport.
2. Declaration at the **Agencia Tributaria Electronic Office** with EORI; TARIC/ CN classification (see the HS reference).
3. Valuation; preference/ origin evidence; documentary/ physical checks.
4. **EU third-country duty + Spanish import VAT (21% standard)**; excises as applicable.
5. Release; free EU circulation.

## Export procedure overview

1. Export declaration; customs exit validation.
2. Dual-use licensing where controlled (SOIVRE restrictions).
3. Preference certificates for FTA destinations; ESTACOM for EU flows.
4. Exit; VAT zero-rating documentation.

## Duty and tax framework

- EU **common external tariff** on CIF; **import VAT 21%** (10%/ 4% reduced); excises on alcohol/ tobacco/ energy.
- Special procedures: customs warehousing, inward processing, **AEO** certification.

## Special requirements and trade programs

- **EORI** via the Agencia (see the Spain entities guide).
- SOIVRE's pre-shipment inspection legacy functions on certain exports.
- Canary Islands' special customs/ tax regime (outside the EU VAT territory for goods).
