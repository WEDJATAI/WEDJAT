# HS Chapter 90 — Optical, photographic, cinematographic, measuring, checking, medical or surgical instruments and apparatus; parts and accessories

Harmonized System **chapter 90** (Section XVIII) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 32 four-digit headings and 143 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
