# HS Chapter 08 — Fruit and nuts, edible; peel of citrus fruit or melons

Harmonized System **chapter 08** (Section II) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 14 four-digit headings and 70 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
