# HS Chapter 89 — Ships, boats and floating structures

Harmonized System **chapter 89** (Section XVII) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 8 four-digit headings and 25 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
