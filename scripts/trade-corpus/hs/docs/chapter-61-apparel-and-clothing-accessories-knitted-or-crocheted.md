# HS Chapter 61 — Apparel and clothing accessories; knitted or crocheted

Harmonized System **chapter 61** (Section XI) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 17 four-digit headings and 106 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
