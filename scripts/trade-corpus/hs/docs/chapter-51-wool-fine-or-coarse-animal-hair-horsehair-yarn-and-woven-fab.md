# HS Chapter 51 — Wool, fine or coarse animal hair; horsehair yarn and woven fabric

Harmonized System **chapter 51** (Section XI) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 13 four-digit headings and 38 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
