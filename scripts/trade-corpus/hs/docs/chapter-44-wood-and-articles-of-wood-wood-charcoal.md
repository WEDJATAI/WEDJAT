# HS Chapter 44 — Wood and articles of wood; wood charcoal

Harmonized System **chapter 44** (Section IX) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 21 four-digit headings and 128 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
