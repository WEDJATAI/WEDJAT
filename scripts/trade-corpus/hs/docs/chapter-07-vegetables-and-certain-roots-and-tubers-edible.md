# HS Chapter 07 — Vegetables and certain roots and tubers; edible

Harmonized System **chapter 07** (Section II) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 14 four-digit headings and 73 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
