# HS Chapter 45 — Cork and articles of cork

Harmonized System **chapter 45** (Section IX) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 4 four-digit headings and 7 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).

## Headings and subheadings

| HS code | Level | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4501 | Heading | Natural cork, raw or simply prepared; waste cork; crushed, granulated or ground cork |
| 450110 | Subheading | Cork; natural cork, raw or simply prepared |
| 450190 | Subheading | Cork; waste cork, crushed, granulated or ground cork |
| 4502 | Heading | Natural cork, debacked or roughly squared, or in rectangular (including square) blocks, plates, sheets or strip, (including sharp-edged blanks for corks or stoppers) |
| 450200 | Subheading | Cork; natural cork, debacked or roughly squared, or in rectangular (including square) blocks, plates, sheets or strip, (including sharp-edged blanks for corks or stoppers) |
| 4503 | Heading | Cork; articles of natural cork |
| 450310 | Subheading | Cork; corks and stoppers, of natural cork |
| 450390 | Subheading | Cork; articles of natural cork other than corks and stoppers |
| 4504 | Heading | Agglomerated cork (with or without a binding substance) and articles of agglomerated cork |
| 450410 | Subheading | Cork; blocks, plates, sheets and strip, tiles of any shape, solid cylinders (including discs), of agglomerated cork (with or without a binding substance) |
| 450490 | Subheading | Cork; articles of agglomerated cork (with or without a binding substance), n.e.c. in heading no. 4504 |
