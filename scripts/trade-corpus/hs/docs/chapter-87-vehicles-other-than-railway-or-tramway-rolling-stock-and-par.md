# HS Chapter 87 — Vehicles; other than railway or tramway rolling stock, and parts and accessories thereof

Harmonized System **chapter 87** (Section XVII) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 16 four-digit headings and 98 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
