# HS Chapter 56 — Wadding, felt and nonwovens, special yarns; twine, cordage, ropes and cables and articles thereof

Harmonized System **chapter 56** (Section XI) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 9 four-digit headings and 30 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
