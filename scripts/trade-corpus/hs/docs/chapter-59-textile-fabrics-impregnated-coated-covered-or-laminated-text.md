# HS Chapter 59 — Textile fabrics; impregnated, coated, covered or laminated; textile articles of a kind suitable for industrial use

Harmonized System **chapter 59** (Section XI) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 11 four-digit headings and 24 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
