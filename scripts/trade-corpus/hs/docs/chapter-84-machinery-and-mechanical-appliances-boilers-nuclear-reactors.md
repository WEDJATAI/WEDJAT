# HS Chapter 84 — Machinery and mechanical appliances, boilers, nuclear reactors; parts thereof

Harmonized System **chapter 84** (Section XVI) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 86 four-digit headings and 538 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
