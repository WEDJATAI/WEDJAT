# HS Chapter 54 — Man-made filaments; strip and the like of man-made textile materials

Harmonized System **chapter 54** (Section XI) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 8 four-digit headings and 72 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
