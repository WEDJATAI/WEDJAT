# HS Chapter 99 — Commodities not specified according to kind

Harmonized System **chapter 99** (Section TOTAL) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 1 four-digit headings and 1 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).

## Headings and subheadings

| 9999 | Heading | Commodities not specified according to kind |
| 999999 | Subheading | Commodities not specified according to kind |
