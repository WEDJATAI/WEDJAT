# HS Chapter 81 — Metals; n.e.c., cermets and articles thereof

Harmonized System **chapter 81** (Section XV) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 12 four-digit headings and 56 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
