# HS Chapter 40 — Rubber and articles thereof

Harmonized System **chapter 40** (Section VII) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 17 four-digit headings and 80 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
