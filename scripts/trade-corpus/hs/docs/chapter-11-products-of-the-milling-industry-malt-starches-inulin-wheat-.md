# HS Chapter 11 — Products of the milling industry; malt, starches, inulin, wheat gluten

Harmonized System **chapter 11** (Section II) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 9 four-digit headings and 27 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
