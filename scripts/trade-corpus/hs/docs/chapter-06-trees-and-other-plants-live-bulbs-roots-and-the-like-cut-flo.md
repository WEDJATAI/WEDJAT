# HS Chapter 06 — Trees and other plants, live; bulbs, roots and the like; cut flowers and ornamental foliage

Harmonized System **chapter 06** (Section II) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 4 four-digit headings and 16 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
