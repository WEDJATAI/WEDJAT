# HS Chapter 38 — Chemical products n.e.c.

Harmonized System **chapter 38** (Section VI) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 27 four-digit headings and 108 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
