# HS Chapter 23 — Food industries, residues and wastes thereof; prepared animal fodder

Harmonized System **chapter 23** (Section IV) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 9 four-digit headings and 23 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
