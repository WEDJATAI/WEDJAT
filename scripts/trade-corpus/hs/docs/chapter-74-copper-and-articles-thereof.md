# HS Chapter 74 — Copper and articles thereof

Harmonized System **chapter 74** (Section XV) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 16 four-digit headings and 49 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
