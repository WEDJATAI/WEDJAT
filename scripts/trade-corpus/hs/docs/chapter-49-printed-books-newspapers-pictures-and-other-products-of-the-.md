# HS Chapter 49 — Printed books, newspapers, pictures and other products of the printing industry; manuscripts, typescripts and plans

Harmonized System **chapter 49** (Section X) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 11 four-digit headings and 18 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
