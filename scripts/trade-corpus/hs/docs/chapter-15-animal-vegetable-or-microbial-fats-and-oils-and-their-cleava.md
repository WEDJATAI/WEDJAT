# HS Chapter 15 — Animal, vegetable or microbial fats and oils and their cleavage products; prepared edible fats; animal or vegetable waxes

Harmonized System **chapter 15** (Section III) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 21 four-digit headings and 53 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
