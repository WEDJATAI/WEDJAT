# HS Chapter 16 — Meat, fish, crustaceans, molluscs or other aquatic invertebrates, or insects; preparations thereof

Harmonized System **chapter 16** (Section IV) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 5 four-digit headings and 42 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
