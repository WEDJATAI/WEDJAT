# HS Chapter 14 — Vegetable plaiting materials; vegetable products not elsewhere specified or included

Harmonized System **chapter 14** (Section II) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 2 four-digit headings and 5 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).

## Headings and subheadings

| HS code | Level | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1401 | Heading | Vegetable materials of a kind used primarily for plaiting; (e.g. bamboos, rattans, reeds, rushes, osier, raffia, cleaned, bleached or dyed cereal straw and lime bark) |
| 140110 | Subheading | Bamboo used primarily for plaiting |
| 140120 | Subheading | Rattans used primarily for plaiting |
| 140190 | Subheading | Vegetable materials of a kind used primarily for plaiting; n.e.c. in heading no. 1401 |
