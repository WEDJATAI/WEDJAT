# HS Chapter 64 — Footwear; gaiters and the like; parts of such articles

Harmonized System **chapter 64** (Section XII) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 6 four-digit headings and 25 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
