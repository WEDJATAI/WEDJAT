# HS Chapter 20 — Preparations of vegetables, fruit, nuts or other parts of plants

Harmonized System **chapter 20** (Section IV) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 9 four-digit headings and 52 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
