# HS Chapter 70 — Glass and glassware

Harmonized System **chapter 70** (Section XIII) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 19 four-digit headings and 71 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
