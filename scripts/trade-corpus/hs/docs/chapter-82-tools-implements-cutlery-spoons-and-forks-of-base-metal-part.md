# HS Chapter 82 — Tools, implements, cutlery, spoons and forks, of base metal; parts thereof, of base metal

Harmonized System **chapter 82** (Section XV) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 15 four-digit headings and 64 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
