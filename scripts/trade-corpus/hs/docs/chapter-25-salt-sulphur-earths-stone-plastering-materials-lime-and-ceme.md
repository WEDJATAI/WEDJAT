# HS Chapter 25 — Salt; sulphur; earths, stone; plastering materials, lime and cement

Harmonized System **chapter 25** (Section V) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 29 four-digit headings and 67 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
