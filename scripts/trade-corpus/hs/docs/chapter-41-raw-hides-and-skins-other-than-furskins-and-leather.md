# HS Chapter 41 — Raw hides and skins (other than furskins) and leather

Harmonized System **chapter 41** (Section VIII) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 11 four-digit headings and 37 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
