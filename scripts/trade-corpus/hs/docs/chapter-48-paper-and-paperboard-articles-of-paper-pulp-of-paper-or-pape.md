# HS Chapter 48 — Paper and paperboard; articles of paper pulp, of paper or paperboard

Harmonized System **chapter 48** (Section X) of the universal 6-digit tariff nomenclature. This chapter contains 22 four-digit headings and 101 six-digit subheadings.

Codes are universal up to 6 digits (WCO Harmonized System); countries extend them to 8–10 digits in national tariff schedules (e.g., US HTS, EU TARIC, Egyptian tariff).
