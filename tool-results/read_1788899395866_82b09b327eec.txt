     4→// WHY THIS SHAPE: the master prompt (§5, §26) requires the hierarchy
     5→//   Organization > Platform > Blueprint > Blueprint Version > Document
     6→//   > Document Version > Section > Chunk > Knowledge Record
     7→// plus model registry / training / evaluation / observability planes.

     9→// SQLite notes: no enums and no arrays are used — lifecycle values are validated
    10→// in the application layer (see src/lib/wedjat/types.ts). Vector payloads and
    11→// structured payloads are stored as JSON text (Turso/libSQL portable).
